## How Kris Actually Fights: Commands, Timing, and Tension

Kris is the party leader: a human from Hometown who wields a melee weapon and, notably, has **no spell list of their own** in the chapters released so far. That single design choice defines how you play the character. All of Kris's value comes from three things — the FIGHT timing bar, the ACT menu, and smart item management.

The FIGHT command works like a swinging meter. A bar sweeps across a target zone, and you press the confirm key as close to the center as possible. A center hit deals maximum damage; a mistimed hit barely scratches. Against weak enemies this hardly matters, but against a boss with thousands of HP, consistently landing near the center is the difference between a clean win and a fifteen-minute grind.

| Command | Effect | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| FIGHT | Timing-based melee attack | Whenever you need dependable damage |
| ACT | Contextual actions like Check, talk, or compliment | To lower enemy defense or open a mercy route |
| ITEM | Uses a consumable on any party member | To heal or top up Tension in an emergency |
| DEFEND | Cuts incoming damage for that turn | During attack patterns you haven't learned yet |
| SPARE | Ends the battle peacefully once the enemy is ready | Pacifist runs and weakened enemies |
| MAGIC | Not available to Kris | Spend Tension on Susie, Ralsei, or Noelle instead |

Tension (TP) is a shared party resource, and Kris is usually the one building it. Grazing — deliberately skimming the edge of incoming bullets rather than dodging wide — is the main way to generate TP without spending a turn. Player experience suggests that new players instinctively run to the far corner of the bullet board, which keeps them safe but leaves the party with almost no Tension for healing or big spells.

Because Kris can't cast anything, your TP decisions are really about everyone else. Susie's damage spells, Ralsei's healing, and Noelle's ice attacks all draw from the same pool Kris helps fill. Treat Kris as the engine and the rest of the party as the payload.

Common mistakes that lose Kris fights:

- **Panic-healing at full Tension.** Spending everything on a scratch wastes the resource you need for a real emergency.
- **Ignoring ACT entirely.** Plenty of fights end faster through dialogue than through damage.
- **Hoarding items.** Consumables you never use are just inventory weight.
- **Skipping DEFEND.** It's the cheapest way to survive a phase you've never seen.
- **Autopiloting the timing bar.** Boss patterns change; your rhythm should too.

**How do I deal more damage with Kris's FIGHT command?**
Focus on hitting the center of the timing bar. Center hits deal significantly more damage than edge hits, and landing them consistently shortens long boss fights more than any single item or spell.

Kris has no personal spell list in the released chapters. Tension is spent through Susie, Ralsei, and Noelle instead, which makes Kris the party's damage and utility backbone rather than its caster.
