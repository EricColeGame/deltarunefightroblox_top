## What Is the "Kris Fight" in Deltarune?

Kris is the human protagonist of Deltarune, a quiet kid in a small town who gets dragged into a Dark World with Susie and Ralsei. The twist is that Kris isn't really "you" — the player commands Kris by directing the red SOUL that lives inside their chest. That separation between character and player is the engine behind the entire Kris fight theory.

The game keeps feeding it. At the end of Chapter 1, Kris quietly pulls out a knife and eats an entire pie in the middle of the night. At the end of Chapter 2, Kris rips the SOUL out of their body, locks it in a cage, and walks toward the door with a knife in hand. Chapter 2's alternate route pushes the discomfort further, with the player directing Noelle to freeze enemies against her will.

| Moment | Chapter | What Happens | Why Fans Read It as Defiance |
|---|---|---|---|
| The knife and the pie | Chapter 1 ending | Kris sneaks downstairs, draws a knife, and devours the whole pie | A private act the player can only watch, not control |
| SOUL removal | Chapter 2 ending | Kris tears out the SOUL and cages it | Reads as Kris physically rejecting player input |
| Snowgrave route | Chapter 2 (alt route) | Player forces Noelle to freeze enemies | Shows how far the player's orders can go |
| Continued ambiguity | Chapters 3–4 (2025) | Kris's autonomy stays unresolved | Keeps the "who is in charge?" question alive |

Community reports consistently point to these scenes as the strongest evidence that a Kris confrontation is being set up rather than simply teased.

## Why Megalovania Keeps Getting Attached

Megalovania isn't originally an Undertale song. Toby Fox wrote it years earlier for his EarthBound Halloween Hack, and it later appeared in Homestuck before becoming Sans's theme in Undertale's Genocide route. That history matters, because the track already carries a very specific meaning: *you* are the villain, and the boss is the one calling you out.

| Era | Where It Appeared | What It Signaled |
|---|---|---|
| Late 2000s | EarthBound Halloween Hack | A fan project theme that spread by word of mouth |
| Early 2010s | Homestuck | Cemented the melody as an internet anthem |
| 2015 | Undertale (Sans fight) | The definitive "the player is the bad guy" battle theme |
| 2018–present | Deltarune fan works | Borrowed for imagined Kris confrontations |

Here's the important caveat: **there is no official Megalovania track in Deltarune.** No released chapter uses it for a Kris encounter, and Toby Fox has not announced one. Every Deltarune Kris fight Megalovania video, remix, or mock-up you see is community-made, which is exactly why the phrase lives in fan spaces rather than official marketing.

**Is Megalovania in Deltarune at all?**
Not in any released chapter. The track's history runs through Toby Fox's earlier projects and Undertale's Genocide route, and it has since become a fandom staple for "the player is the villain" scenarios.

Fan game hosting sites and video platforms are the main hubs, and quality varies a lot. Look for projects with recent updates, clear creator credits, and community discussion before downloading anything.

**Why do fans think Kris will eventually fight the player?**
The Chapter 1 and Chapter 2 endings show Kris acting independently, including removing the SOUL entirely. Community reports treat those scenes as setup for a confrontation, though nothing has been confirmed by the developer.
