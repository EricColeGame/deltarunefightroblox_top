## The TP Economy: Where Kris-Led Runs Are Won

TP (Tension Points) is the resource that powers every spell in Deltarune, and it is shared across the party. You build TP by grazing enemy bullets during the enemy's turn — passing close to projectiles without getting hit — and by playing defensively when a party member has nothing useful to do.

This is where most boss attempts fall apart. Players burn TP on flashy spells early, then have nothing left for the healing they desperately need at 30% boss health.

| Action | TP Effect | When to Use It |
|---|---|---|
| Grazing bullets | Builds TP steadily | Every enemy turn — always be grazing |
| Defensive turns | Preserves HP, builds TP | When the boss telegraphs a big attack |
| Rude Buster / damage spells | Spends TP for burst | Only when a boss is close to a phase change |
| Heal Prayer | Spends TP for recovery | Immediately after a big hit, not before |
| Pacify | Spends TP to end a fight | When the enemy is already tired or weakened |

A simple rule: never drop below enough TP for one emergency heal. If you can't afford a heal, you can't afford a Rude Buster.

## Step-by-Step: How to Win a Kris-Led Boss Fight

If you are stuck, run this checklist before you blame your reflexes.

1. **Spend your first turn on Check.** Knowing the enemy's stats and spare conditions tells you whether this is a damage fight or a mercy fight.
2. **Identify the win condition.** Some bosses end through ACT and SPARE; others only fall to damage. Attacking a spare-only boss wastes turns.
3. **Assign roles before the fight starts.** Decide who heals, who attacks, and who builds TP. Switching mid-fight costs turns.
4. **Graze every single turn.** Free TP is free healing. Skipping grazes because you are focused on dodging is the most common self-inflicted mistake.
5. **Bank a heal.** Keep enough TP for one Heal Prayer at all times. This single habit prevents most wipes.
6. **Learn the phase change.** Most bosses escalate at set health thresholds. Save burst damage for the moment after a phase shift, not before.
7. **Do not rush the final 20%.** Panic attacking is how a winning run becomes a game over.

## Pacifist vs. Weird Route: Two Different Fights

The same boss can be a completely different encounter depending on the route you are on. In the standard Pacifist path, the party talks, ACTs, and spares its way through. In the alternate route — often called the Weird Route or Snowgrave path — Noelle's abilities become the centerpiece and Kris's commands push the party into far darker territory.

| Element | Pacifist Route | Alternate (Snowgrave) Route |
|---|---|---|
| Win condition | Spare the boss | Overwhelm with ice-based damage |
| TP priority | Healing and Pacify | Noelle's offensive spells |
| Difficulty | Moderate, forgiving | Higher, with fewer safety nets |
| Kris's role | Diplomat and coordinator | Commander issuing uncomfortable orders |

If you are following a **deltarune kris fight** strategy from a video or forum post, check which route it assumes. Advice for one route can actively sabotage the other.

**Is there an actual boss named Kris in Deltarune?**

**Which Kris-led boss fight is the hardest?**
Community consensus usually points to the secret bosses — Jevil in Chapter 1 and Spamton NEO in Chapter 2 — as the toughest early walls. Both demand precise grazing, careful TP budgeting, and patience over aggression.

**Should I attack or spare in Kris's boss fights?**
Check the enemy first. Some encounters resolve cleanly through ACT and SPARE, while others require sustained damage. The Check command exists specifically to tell you which is which.

**Does the route change how Kris fights?**
Yes. In the standard Pacifist route, Kris coordinates the party and opens mercy options. In the alternate route, Kris's commands push the party toward far more aggressive, ice-focused tactics with less room for error.
