## Who Are Kris and Susie?

Kris is the silent protagonist — the "vessel" the player controls. They never speak a line of dialogue, live with Toriel, and have a habit of doing unsettling things once the lights go out. Susie is the school bully who gets dragged into the Dark World alongside them, swinging an axe first and asking questions never.

Their dynamic is the engine of the whole story. Susie starts the game treating Kris as a punching bag and ends it treating them as the one person she trusts. That reversal is why the Deltarune Kris fight Susie idea feels so loaded.

## FAQ

**Does Kris ever fight Susie in Deltarune?**
Not in a full boss battle. The closest moments are the Chapter 1 hallway confrontation and Susie's near-attack on Ralsei, both of which resolve without combat. Any true Deltarune Kris fight Susie duel remains community speculation rather than confirmed content.

**Can you attack Susie in battle?**
No. Susie is a party member, and Deltarune's battle commands only target enemies. There is no friendly-fire option in the standard game.

**Is there a secret Susie boss fight in any chapter?**
There is no official one. Fan-made mods and unofficial web ports have added custom battles, but these are community projects and are not part of Toby Fox's release.

**Why do fans want a Kris fight Susie scene so badly?**
Because their friendship is the emotional anchor of the series. Susie's growth from bully to loyal friend depends entirely on trusting Kris, so a betrayal would be the single biggest twist the story could deliver.
